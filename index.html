      What to practise

      • Hug corners. Every bit of slack you leave is distance the chaser gets for free.
      • Read the red glow. It shines ahead of the chaser even when a wall hides them. If it turns around, so should you.
      • Running leaves scratch marks. The chaser hunts by sight: it chases when it sees you or a scratch mark, and follows the freshest mark it can see. Walk and the trail goes cold.
      • Run straight at windows for about a metre to get the fast vault. Angled approaches are slow.
      • Three vaults jams a window. Leave after two.
      • Don't over-stay. The chaser builds momentum (speed) the longer a chase goes without a hit, a stun or a pallet break.
      • Pallets are a budget. Loop until the last safe lap, then drop it. Stuns are a bonus, not the goal.
